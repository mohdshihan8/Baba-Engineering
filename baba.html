About Us

Who is Baba Engineering?

Baba Engineering is a trusted name in structural steel fabrication and welding. We take on contracts of all sizes — from small custom gates to large industrial truss systems — delivering every project on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.

With a skilled team of certified welders and fabricators, state-of-the-art equipment, and years of hands-on experience, we are the go-to partner for builders, contractors, industries, and homeowners across the region.
